Introduction:
The first segment is only a guide, 

- It involves movement and getting bandages, oxygen tanks.
- Talking to NPCs.
- Talking to an NPC usually gives you the quests that are under the "CAPS LOCK" key, they direct you to what you need to do at a given moment.
- Checking how to shoot.
- Seeing that you have a helmet that automatically opens when you are outside because the whole action takes place on Mars.
- When you are outside your oxygen decreases, when the oxygen reaches the critical level = 0 you start to slowly lose health points.
- When you are indoors you are in a place where there is oxygen and you do not need a helmet to provide you with oxygen outside.
- Checking how the ammo pack works.
- Beware of zombies.
- You can exchange weapons for other better/worse ones.
- You can check how grenades work, there are 2 types, smoke and explosive, each of them has an individual feature.
- Card system to open doors.
- By pressing the "T" button you have a vision of an eagle that highlights interesting places, such as grenades, ammo packs, bandages, quests etc.
- MarsHurricane system for fog and much more.

===================== THE GOAL ==================
To get to the next segment you need to go up the stairs, 
open the bridge and cross the bridge to pick up the green card which you need to open the main hangar.
